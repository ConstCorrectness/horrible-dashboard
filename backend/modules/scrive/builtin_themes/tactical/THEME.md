# Tactical

An engineering log with the dashboard's look: dark, gridded, amber accent rules,
uppercase display headings, monospace metadata.

Voice: terse and technical. Write the way a good incident report reads: what changed,
why, what it cost, what to watch. Numbers beat adjectives. Short headings that are
nouns ("Results", "The fix") read best in uppercase.

Layout notes for writers:

- Headings render uppercase with wide tracking, so keep them to a few words.
- Every post card on the home page shows its date, tags and description in mono. A
  description of one line, under 140 characters, fits the card without wrapping.
- Code, tables and figures are framed with a 2px accent top rule; they are first-class
  here. A table of results is welcome.
- The title comes from the frontmatter. Do not repeat it as a `# Heading`.
