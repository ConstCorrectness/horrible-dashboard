# Article

One long-form piece: an essay, a paper, a technical report. The site's `index.md` is
the article; other pages are listed at its end under "More".

Voice: careful and argued. State the claim in the opening paragraph, then build it
section by section. Every claim that rests on someone else's work gets a citation or a
link; every equation is introduced in words before it appears and explained after.

Layout notes for writers:

- `##` and `###` headings are numbered automatically (1, 1.1…). Do not number them in
  the text.
- Display equations are numbered too. Give the ones you refer to a `:label:` and refer
  to them with a cross-reference rather than "the equation above".
- Footnotes collect at the end; use them for asides, not for sources a reader needs.
- Serif body type at a long measure: paragraphs of four to six sentences read best.
- The title comes from the frontmatter. Do not repeat it as a `# Heading`.
