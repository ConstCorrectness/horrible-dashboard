A devlog is personal and short: one screen to read, written in the first person.

- Lead with the most interesting change, not the chronology.
- Show, don't list: a screenshot, GIF or short clip beats a paragraph for anything
  visual (embed media from the site's `media/` folder).
- The "learned" section is the part people share: one real problem, the wrong turn,
  and the fix — specific enough that someone else could avoid it.
- Keep "next" honest and small. No dates you will not keep.
