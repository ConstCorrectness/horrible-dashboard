A review is useful when it is precise about the paper and honest about its limits.

- Link the paper in the lead. Quote headline numbers exactly, with their baseline.
- Distinguish **what the authors claim**, **what their experiments show**, and **your
  opinion** — label opinion as such.
- Put results in a table (`| method | metric | … |`), not a paragraph of numbers.
- If the paper's argument rests on one equation, show it as display math and explain
  every symbol in the sentence after it.
- Never invent results, citations or quotes. If you have not read a section, say what
  you are unsure of instead of guessing.
